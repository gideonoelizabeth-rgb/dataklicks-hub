What You Will Learn to Build.

Five worked projects by our founder, Elizabeth Gideon, each answering a real business question with Excel, SQL, Power BI or Python. They show the kind of analysis you practise in our classes.

About the data. Every dataset on this page is sample (synthetic) data created for training, using a fixed random seed so each number can be reproduced. No employer or client data is used. The figures describe the sample data, not real businesses.

Retail sales performance dashboard

The question: which regions, product categories and sales channels drive revenue and profit across a year of orders?

  • 3,000 orders in 2025 produced ₦308.0M in revenue and ₦71.9M in profit, a 23.4% margin.
  • Online channels account for 49.9% of revenue, almost half.
  • Lagos is the biggest region (₦120.2M). Electronics brings the most revenue (₦174.3M) but earns a 16.4% margin, while Beauty earns 46.8%.
  • Monthly revenue climbs from ₦17.0M in January to ₦38.3M in December.

Marketplace orders and vendor analysis

The question: how fast is an online marketplace growing, and how dependent is it on a few vendors?

  • Value of delivered orders grows from ₦4.5M in January to ₦28.6M in December, peaking at ₦33.4M in October.
  • The top 20% of vendors generate 66.3% of the value.
  • 64.7% of buyers (1,854 of 2,867) ordered at least twice.
  • Of customers who made a first order in a given month, about 40% to 53% ordered again the following month.

Bank customer churn analysis

The question: which customers are likely to leave, and what do they have in common?

  • Of 6,000 customers, 16.1% churned.
  • Churn is 21.4% for customers without the mobile app and 12.3% for app users.
  • Customers holding one product churn at 22.1%, those holding four at 4.5%. Two or more complaints in a year means 36.2% churn.
  • A simple model (AUC 0.715) places 42.7% of all churners in the highest-risk 20% of customers.

Microfinance loan portfolio dashboard

The question: how healthy is a loan portfolio, and which branches carry the most risk?

  • 4,200 loans: ₦4.94B disbursed and ₦2.67B still outstanding.
  • Portfolio at risk over 30 days (PAR30) is 6.93% overall.
  • By branch, PAR30 ranges from 4.36% (Lekki) to 10.77% (Ikorodu).
  • Built on a star schema with DAX measures such as PAR30 %, month-on-month change and year-to-date.

Paid social campaign performance

The question: which advertising campaigns earn their budget back?

  • ₦13.57M of spend returned ₦52.73M in revenue: ₦3.89 for every ₦1 spent, from 2,599 purchases.
  • Brand Search Promo returned ₦13.62 per ₦1 and Retargeting (cart) ₦11.85.
  • Prospecting (video) returned ₦0.71 per ₦1, so it lost money.
