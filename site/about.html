About DataKlicks Hub

Building a More Data-Literate and AI-Ready Africa.

DataKlicks Hub is a technology, education and business solutions company committed to equipping individuals and organizations with the skills, tools and knowledge required to thrive in a rapidly changing digital economy.

Explore Our Work
Our Story

From a Lagos Startup to an African Digital Skills Company.

DataKlicks Hub was founded in 2023 with a clear mission: to make practical data education more accessible and equip individuals with skills relevant to the changing world of work.

What began as a data analytics training startup focused on Microsoft Excel, Power BI, Tableau, SQL and Python has grown into a broader vision — encompassing business analytics, artificial intelligence, automation, digital skills and business consulting.

Our approach goes beyond classroom instruction. We believe people learn best when they can practice, build, solve and apply. That is why our learning experiences emphasize practical projects, real-world datasets, business scenarios and career preparation.

For organizations, we help turn data and technology into practical business value through training, analytics and consulting.

Our Evolution

Milestones on the Journey.

From a data analytics startup to a growing African technology, education and business solutions brand.

2023

DataKlicks Hub founded in Lagos, Nigeria — beginning with a focus on data analytics and business analysis education.

2024

Digital literacy and data skills initiative launched with secondary school students — extending our reach beyond the classroom.

2025

Public expansion of the initiative toward 3,000 students, reported by Tribune Online. Growing programmes in AI literacy and corporate training.

2026 →

Continued expansion into data, AI education, automation and business solutions across Africa.

Founder & Leadership

Elizabeth Gideon — Founder

Elizabeth Gideon is a data analyst, educator, business strategist and technology entrepreneur focused on helping individuals and businesses make better use of data, technology and digital skills.

She founded DataKlicks Hub with a vision of making practical data education more accessible while creating pathways for people to develop relevant skills for the future of work. Her professional journey spans banking, customer service, sales, data analytics, business strategy and entrepreneurship — bringing together technical understanding and business experience.
