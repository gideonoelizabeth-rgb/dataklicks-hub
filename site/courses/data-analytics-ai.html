Data Analytics & AI

Build practical skills in data analysis, visualization and business problem-solving with Excel, Power BI and the application of AI tools.

Enrol Now · ₦500,000 Ask About the Next Start Date

Who it is for

  • Beginners and career switchers who want a first role in data.
  • Professionals who work with spreadsheets and reports and want to analyse them properly.
  • Business owners and managers who want to make decisions from their own data.

What you will be able to do

  • Clean, organise and analyse data in Excel.
  • Build dashboards and charts that make the answer easy to see.
  • Turn a business question into an analysis and explain your findings clearly.
  • Use AI tools to speed up your analysis, and check what they give you.

What the course covers

  1. Working with data in ExcelFormulas, cleaning, summarising and pivot tables.
  2. Analysis and business problem-solvingTurning a question into an analysis and a recommendation.
  3. Visualisation and dashboards in Power BIModels, measures and clear dashboards.
  4. Applying AI toolsUsing AI to explain, check and speed up your work responsibly.
  5. ProjectsApply what you learn to realistic datasets and present your findings.

Microsoft Excel, Power BI and AI tools. You will also see how these fit with SQL, which has its own course.
