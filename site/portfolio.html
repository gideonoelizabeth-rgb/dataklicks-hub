About Me

Data, AI & a little tech baddie energy.

Hi, I'm Elizabeth Gideon — a Data & AI Coach, educator, and technology entrepreneur. I help individuals build future-ready careers in data and AI, and support businesses in using data and digital tools to grow.

My work spans coaching, corporate training, community speaking, and building initiatives that expand access to technology — especially for women and girls across Africa.

I believe technology is most powerful when it is understandable, practical, and put in the hands of people ready to build.

— Elizabeth
