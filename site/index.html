CSR · Impact

Taking Digital Skills Beyond the Classroom.

In 2024, DataKlicks Hub began an initiative to introduce secondary school students to digital literacy and practical data skills. Through hands-on learning experiences, students are introduced to digital literacy, data awareness, Microsoft Excel, problem-solving and the possibilities that technology can create.

We believe digital education should not be reserved for people who can afford expensive training. The earlier young people are introduced to technology, the more possibilities they can imagine for themselves.
