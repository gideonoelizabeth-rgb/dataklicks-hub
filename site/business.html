What We Do for Businesses

Business Automations

  • Follow-ups and reminders that happen without anyone having to remember
  • Faster, consistent replies to customer enquiries, with your team in control
  • Reports and spreadsheet updates that build themselves
  • Your everyday tools connected, so information flows between them

How We Work

Tailor-Made, Starting With a Consultation.

Every business is different, so we do not sell off-the-shelf packages or publish fixed prices. We start by understanding your business, then propose a solution and a quote that fit it.

1. Consultation

You tell us about your business, your challenges and what you want to improve. We ask questions about your processes, tools and data.

2. Understanding

We study how your business works today, so we recommend what will really help rather than what is fashionable.

3. Proposal

We send a tailor-made proposal with what we will do, how, and what it will cost for your specific needs.

4. Delivery

We build, train or analyse as agreed, and keep you informed along the way.

5. Review

We check that it works for your team and agree any next steps.

Questions

Common Questions.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your business and what you need, so we do not publish a price list. After the consultation we prepare a proposal and a quote that fit your situation.

What happens in a consultation?

We learn about your business: what you do, how work flows through your team, the tools and data you use, and what is slowing you down. Then we tell you honestly what could help and what we would suggest first.

We are a small business. Can you still help?

Yes. Many useful improvements are small and simple. We will recommend a sensible starting point for your size and budget.

Do you only build automations, or can you train our staff and analyse our data too?

All four: automations, staff training, data analytics and digital solutions. You can use one service or combine several.

What about the safety of our data?

We agree with you up front what we can access and how it is handled. We also advise against putting confidential company or customer data into public AI tools unless it is allowed, and we design solutions with that in mind.
